### 👋 About Me

I'm a Full Stack Developer with a strong Linux foundation. I like to keep things simple and focused — tackle the core functionality first, then refine the details carefully. That mindset pairs naturally with languages like **Go**, **Java**, and **TypeScript**.

Over the past few years I've shipped RESTful APIs with authentication & role management, integrated Google Cloud services into backend analytics pipelines, designed clean dashboards for data visualization, and built JavaScript-based mobile apps that talk to multiple microservices.

Outside of work: gaming, reading tech articles, and a cup of green tea in front of Netflix — right before I open the editor again.

### 🔭 Currently Building

Rebuilding my personal portfolio with a heavy focus on the animation layer — **SvelteKit 2 + Svelte 5 + TypeScript (strict)**, a custom **WebGL orb** written in Three.js with hand-rolled GLSL shaders (simplex-noise displacement, fresnel rim lighting, chromatic aberration), synced to a **GSAP ScrollSmoother**-driven scroll experience.

👉 Live at **[faisalramadhanid.netlify.app](https://faisalramadhanid.netlify.app/)**
